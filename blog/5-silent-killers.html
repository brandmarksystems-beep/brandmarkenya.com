5 Silent Brand Killers Killing SMEs in Eldoret

Published Aug 2025 | By BrandMark Kenya

1. Inconsistent Logo & Colors

Using different logos on WhatsApp, posters, and receipts confuses customers. Your brand should look the same everywhere.

2. No Clear Message

If customers can't tell what you sell in 3 seconds, they leave. "We sell everything" is not a brand.

3. Bad Website on Mobile

80% of your customers in Eldoret are on phone. If your site loads slow or looks broken, you lose them.

4. Ignoring Google Reviews

No reviews = no trust. Ask 5 happy customers this week to leave you a Google review.

5. Copying Competitors

Be different. What makes YOUR business in Eldoret special? Tell that story.
