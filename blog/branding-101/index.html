Branding 101: How to Look Professional on a Budget

August 24, 2026 | By BrandMark Team

You don’t need KSh 100,000 to look professional. Most SMEs in Eldoret are losing customers because they *look* cheap, not because they *are* cheap.

1. Get One Good Logo

Stop using 5 different versions of your logo. Pick one, get it in high-res, and use it everywhere. A clean logo = instant trust.

2. Pick 2 Brand Colors and Stick to Them

Your WhatsApp DP, flyers, and receipts should have the same 2 colors. It makes you memorable. Look at Safaricom Green. Simple.

3. Fix Your WhatsApp Business Profile

This is your shopfront in 2026. Add logo, description, catalog, and business hours. 80% of Eldoret customers will check this first.

4. Use Templates for Consistency

Use Canva with your colors and logo saved. Every post should look like it came from the same business.

5. Speak With One Voice

Are you formal or friendly? Pick it. Don’t be "Dear esteemed customer" on Monday and "Yo bro" on Tuesday.

Professionalism is not about money. It’s about consistency.
