1. Embroidery vs Printing: What Lasts in the Field?

For shirts, blouses, and corporate vests, we use industrial embroidery, not heat press. Why? Our 12-head embroidery machine does 1000 stitches per minute with Madeira Polyneon threads that don't fade in Uasin Gishu sun or peel after washing. Printing is okay for events, but for daily wear, embroidery wins.

2. The 3 Things That Make a Shirt Look Cheap

Wrong fabric: We source 100% cotton and Lacoste piqué that doesn't shrink.

Thin stitch count: We use 8,000-12,000 stitches for a chest logo, not 4,000.

Wrong placement: We measure 10cm below collar, perfectly centered.
