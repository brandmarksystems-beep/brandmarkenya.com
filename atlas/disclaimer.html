In short. Every figure on Atlas is an estimate. Prices, shipping, duty, levies, VAT and delivery times can differ from what you actually pay. Confirm with the seller, your carrier and a licensed clearing agent or the Kenya Revenue Authority before you buy.

Estimates, not quotes

The “landed cost” shown for each item adds the item price, estimated freight, fuel surcharge, handling, insurance, import duty, levies and VAT. We calculate it from rates we set and update, using the weight and size you enter or that we assume. It is a planning guide. It is not an offer, a quote or a promise of the final cost.

Listing prices

  • Illustrative listings are examples to show how Atlas works. They are not real current offers.
  • Local store prices (Jumia, Jiji, Kilimall and supermarkets) are shown as shelf prices in Kenyan shillings. They already include VAT and duty, so Atlas adds nothing. Delivery fees, offers and stock vary by store and branch.
  • Verified listings were checked against the seller’s page on the date shown. Prices, stock and specifications can change at any time after that, and we cannot see every seller’s changes. An amber date means the check is getting old.
  • Live search results, where switched on, are read automatically from the public pages of other stores a moment before you see them. They are not checked by us. They can be out of date, incomplete or wrong, so always confirm the price and the item on the store’s own site.
  • Prices are converted at exchange rates from a third-party provider. The rate your bank, card or seller applies will differ.

Customs, duty and tax

Duty depends on how customs classifies your goods (the HS code), where they are made, any exemptions or licences, and rules in force on the day of import. Our category rates are indicative averages, not a classification. VAT, Import Declaration Fee, Railway Development Levy and other charges change through Finance Acts and notices. Customs may value goods differently from the invoice. Atlas does not include every possible charge, such as excise duty, port or storage fees, inspection, agent fees, permits or last-mile delivery to your door unless stated.

Restricted and regulated goods

Some goods need approval or are prohibited or restricted, for example radio and telecom equipment (Communications Authority type approval), goods subject to KEBS or other standards, pharmaceuticals, food, chemicals and batteries. A product appearing on Atlas does not mean it is legal or permitted to import. It is your responsibility to check.

Shipping and delivery

Transit and clearance times are typical ranges, not guarantees. Delays arise from carriers, airlines, shipping lines, weather, inspections and public holidays. Chargeable weight, the dimensional factor and minimum charges vary by carrier. Use your own carrier’s or forwarder’s rate card for a real figure.
